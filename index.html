01 / PROFILE

About

Academic role and professional information.

Dr. Chandra Naik is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Alva's Institute of Engineering and Technology.

His academic interests span computer science, algorithms, artificial intelligence, optimization and data science. His teaching portfolio includes foundational computing subjects as well as advanced topics such as compiler design and game theory.
